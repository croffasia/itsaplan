# Plan an issue

## Set up a team and project
Type "alex@example.com" as the Email, and "Plan-Test-2026" as both the Password and the Confirm password, then click "Create account".
Click "Create team", type "Morgan Labs" as the Team name and click "Create team" in the dialog.
Click "Create project", type "Website Launch" as the Name and click "Create project" in the dialog.
Click "Got it" on the "What's new" panel.

## Create an issue
Click the "New issue" button in the top bar.
Type "Design the landing page" as the Issue title.
Click "Backlog" and choose "Todo", then click "Priority" and choose "High".
Click "Create issue".
Verify "WL-1" "Design the landing page" is shown in the Todo column.

## Start work on it
Click "Design the landing page" to open the issue.
Verify its Priority is "High".
Click the State "Todo" and choose "In Progress".
Close the issue panel.
Verify "Design the landing page" is shown in the In Progress column.
